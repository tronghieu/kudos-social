# Kudos Social 👏

A lightweight social network where people recognize each other through kudos.

## Why
Teams and communities move faster when appreciation is visible, frequent, and specific. Kudos Social makes it easy to:
- Give kudos in seconds
- Celebrate wins publicly (or within a group)
- Build a culture of recognition over time

## Core Features (MVP)
- **Kudos feed**: a timeline of recent kudos
- **Give kudos**: mention a person, write a message, add tags (e.g. `helpful`, `leadership`)
- **Reactions & comments**: keep it simple and positive
- **Leaderboard**: so top 5 receiver kudos
- **Profiles**: see a person’s received kudos, top tags, and highlights

## Product Principles
- **Positive-by-design**: encourage gratitude, reduce toxicity vectors
- **Low friction**: quick, lightweight interactions
- **Meaningful signals**: emphasize context and specificity over vanity metrics

## UI Style

- Gamification & Colorful
